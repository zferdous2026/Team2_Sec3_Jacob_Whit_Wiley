# Economic Effect of Coal Mines
## Team2_Sec3_Joseph_Whit_Wiley
### This app aims to examine the relationship between coal mines and the economic state of the counties they are present in. The economic indicators utilized include county GDP, individual spending, and unemployment. Indicators are sourced from the Bureau of Economic Analysis. The coal mine data is from the U.S Geological survey including coal mine locations. The collected data will be used for an interactive map to display counties with and without coal mines with each counties economic state. Additional scatterplots will be produced to display direct relationships between economic factors and coal mines. The overarching question the data set is answering is do coal mines hinder the local economies they are present in, and if so why? Is it another example of the paradox of plenty?
